01 / The morning

One late change can ripple through the whole run.

At 6:40, a stop changes. A student needs an accessible vehicle. A bus is already near capacity. The dispatcher has a paper sheet, a spreadsheet and a phone that won’t stop ringing.

YottaFlex models that everyday pressure as a connected operation: see who is assigned, which routes are workable, and what needs attention before the doors open.

05 / The operating principles

Grounded in the constraints of the route.

A transport workflow is only useful when the numbers, privacy boundaries and decisions are easy to inspect. The demo keeps those assumptions in the open.

01 / DECISIONS

Rules you can read.

Assignment and route checks are deterministic. Scenario facts stay separate from evaluation ground truth.

02 / PRIVACY

Safe by construction.

All records are synthetic. Contact details are placeholders and sensitive address fields are masked in ordinary views.

03 / REALITY

Constraints included.

Capacity, accessibility, stop dwell and service duration all factor into the operational model.
